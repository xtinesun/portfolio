02 · Lab

Building with Claude.

  • Listening…

    In progress · Proof of concept · iOS · Voice AI

    Audire

    Audire gives AI an ear and a voice through Siri, starting with Claude, straight from your iPhone with no separate hardware to buy. Say “Hey Siri, ask Claude,” speak, and hear Claude answer back.

    Read more
    What it taught me
    In my first build, I couldn’t tell if Audire was actually listening. Designing and coding at the same time caught that missing “listening” indicator, a gap I’d normally only find after real users tried it.
    How I’m building it
    Feasibility first. I’m coding and testing the core loop (call, speak, hear back) in Xcode with Claude. Once the proof of concept works, I’ll connect Claude to Figma through MCP to design the UX properly. If it doesn’t, I fail fast and move to the next idea.
    What’s next
    Expanding beyond the proof of concept to support other AI agents, using iOS Shortcuts as a fallback to work around API limitations.

03 · About

Hi, I’m Christine.

I’m a product designer in Irvine, a mom of two, and a goldendoodle’s favorite person. I trained as a graphic designer at RISD, and I fell for product design because of the messy beginning: the part where nobody has the answer yet and every opinion is in the room. Off-screen, you’ll find me at the pottery wheel, in an escape room, or down a research rabbit hole with a spreadsheet for everything.

How I work

  • Five opinions, one direction. I run workshops and crits that turn a debate into a decision people actually feel good about.
  • PM and engineering are my partners, not my audience. I co-wrote strategy, sat in on pricing, and hand off designs engineers can build without guessing.
  • I go talk to people. I ran my own creator sessions alongside formal research, because the best insights show up in the side comments.
  • I build to find out. I prototype in code with Claude before the debate gets long, so we decide with something real in our hands.

Want to see how I work? I’ll walk you through it.
