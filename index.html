02 · Lab

Fall 2026 → now. Things I’m building on my own, with Claude and Figma.

With Claude, the gap between designing and building has nearly closed.

I move back and forth between Figma, Claude, and code. Building it myself shows me where people would get stuck, long before a real user has to.

  • Listening…

    In progress · Proof of concept · iOS · Voice AI

    Musear

    Musear gives Muse, an AI assistant, an ear and a voice, straight from your iPhone with no separate hardware to buy. Say “Hey Muse,” speak, and hear Muse answer back.

    Read more
    What it taught me
    In my first build, I couldn’t tell if Musear was actually listening. Designing and coding at the same time caught that missing “listening” indicator, a gap I’d normally only find after real users tried it.
    How I’m building it
    Feasibility first. I’m coding and testing the core loop (call, speak, hear back) in Xcode with Claude. Once the proof of concept works, I’ll connect Claude to Figma through MCP to design the UX properly. If it doesn’t, I fail fast and move to the next idea.

03 · About

Who you’d be working with.

Hi, I’m Christine.

I’m a product designer in Irvine, a mom of two, and a goldendoodle’s favorite person. I trained as a graphic designer at RISD, and I fell for product design because of the messy beginning: the part where nobody has the answer yet and every opinion is in the room. Off-screen, you’ll find me at the pottery wheel, in an escape room, or down a research rabbit hole, creating a spreadsheet for everything.

How I work

  • Five opinions, one direction. I run workshops and crits that turn a debate into a decision people actually feel good about.
  • PM and engineering are my partners, not my audience. I co-wrote strategy, sat in on pricing, and hand off designs engineers can build without guessing.
  • I go talk to people. I ran my own creator sessions alongside formal research, because the best insights show up in the side comments.
  • I build to find out. I prototype in code with Claude before the debate gets long, so we decide with something real in our hands.

Want to see how I work? I’ll walk you through it.
